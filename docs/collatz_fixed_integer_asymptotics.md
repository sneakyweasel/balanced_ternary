# Fixed-integer asymptotic affine-center geometry

This note records Milestone 10. Claim labels are **PROVED**, **VERIFIED
COMPUTATIONALLY**, **CONJECTURE**, and **OBSERVATION**. Nothing here is a
proof or disproof of the Collatz conjecture. The OEIS / warp branch is
left frozen: no new balanced-ternary maps are introduced.

The central object is one **fixed** positive odd integer \(n\) together
with the infinite sequence of exact affine equations generated by its
actual accelerated itinerary.

## Fixed-integer viewpoint

Write \(n_0=n\) and \(n_{m+1}=T(n_m)\). The prefix
\(\mathbf{k}=(k_0,\ldots,k_{m-1})\) is the actual valuation word of \(n\),
so the cylinder representative of every such prefix is \(n\) itself:

\[
n\equiv R(\mathbf{k})\pmod{2^{K_m+1}}.
\]

Once \(2^{K_m+1}>n\), uniqueness forces \(R_m=n\). Finite-code diagnostics
that vary \(R_m\) over all words of a given length are a different
experiment.

The exact state stored by `InfiniteTrajectoryAffineState` is

\[
\bigl(n,\,m,\,K_m,\,C_m,\,x_m=T^m(n),\,\lambda_m=3^m/2^{K_m},\,
n_{*m},\,n-n_{*m},\,G_m,\,A_m\bigr),
\]

with every rational in exact `Fraction` arithmetic.

## Equivalent normal forms

From \(2^{K_m}x_m=3^m n+C_m\) the following are **PROVED** and mutually
equivalent:

- (A) \(x_m=\lambda_m n+C_m/2^{K_m}\)
- (B) \(x_m-n_{*m}=\lambda_m(n-n_{*m})\) whenever \(2^{K_m}\ne3^m\)
- (C) \(A_m:=C_m/3^m=\lambda_m^{-1}x_m-n\)
- (D) \(C_m/2^{K_m}=x_m-\lambda_m n\)
- (E) \(n_{*m}=C_m/(2^{K_m}-3^m)\)
- (F) \(n-n_{*m}=(2^{K_m}n-3^m n-C_m)/(2^{K_m}-3^m)\)

The integer affine gap

\[
\boxed{
G_m=n(2^{K_m}-3^m)-C_m=2^{K_m}(n-x_m)
}
\]

is **PROVED** and **LEAN VERIFIED**. Consequently

\[
G_m>0\iff x_m<n,\qquad
G_m=0\iff x_m=n,\qquad
G_m<0\iff x_m>n.
\]

This is the orbit-versus-start comparison. It is **not** positivity
\(x_m\ge1\). Lean records an explicit witness: the first step of \(7\) is
\(11\), so \(x\ge1\) holds while \(G<0\).

## Recurrences

With \(C_0=0\), \(K_0=0\), \(G_0=0\),

\[
C_{m+1}=3C_m+2^{K_m},\qquad
K_{m+1}=K_m+k_m,
\]

\[
\boxed{
G_{m+1}=3G_m+2^{K_m}\bigl(n(2^{k_m}-3)-1\bigr).
}
\]

The addend is strictly negative for \(k_m=1\) and nonnegative for
\(k_m\ge2\) when \(n\ge1\). This recurrence is **PROVED** and **LEAN
VERIFIED**. It does not by itself force \(G_m\ge0\) on actual orbits:
every \(n\equiv3\pmod4\) begins with \(k_0=1\) and \(G_1=-n-1<0\).

The normalized contribution \(A_m=C_m/3^m\) obeys

\[
A_{m+1}=A_m+\frac{2^{K_m}}{3^{m+1}},
\qquad
A_m=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\frac{2^{K_j}}{3^{j+1}}.
\]

Equality of the closed form with the series is **PROVED** (and checked
in the implementation). Finite-\(m\) bounds that do not assume an average
slope are

\[
\sum_{j<m}3^{-(j+1)}\le A_m\le 2^{K_{m-1}}\sum_{j<m}3^{-(j+1)},
\]

because \(0\le K_j\le K_{m-1}\). Average slope \(K_m/m\) alone does not
decide convergence of the series: the terms are controlled by the whole
path \(K_j\).

The successor center is the exact rational

\[
n_{*,m+1}=\frac{3C_m+2^{K_m}}{2^{K_m+k_m}-3^{m+1}}
\]

whenever the denominator is nonzero.

## Orbit decomposition

Define \(B_m=(2^{K_m}/3^m)x_m\). Then **PROVED**

\[
B_m=n+A_m.
\]

Positivity \(x_m\ge1\) restates as \(B_m\ge2^{K_m}/3^m\). On an actual
orbit this is a tautology. It contains no extra itinerary information
beyond \(x_m\ge1\).

**Part 19.** \(A_m\) is the same information as the exponent code's
\(C_m/3^m\), rewritten as a series. It is not a new coordinate.

## Regime geometry for a fixed start

Let \(D_m=2^{K_m}-3^m\). Unique factorization gives \(D_m\ne0\) for
\(m\ge1\) (**PROVED; LEAN VERIFIED**).

- Expanding \(D_m<0\): \(C_m>0\) forces \(G_m<0\), hence \(x_m>n\) and
  \(n_{*m}<0<n\). So \(n_{*m}\le n\) holds automatically.
- Contracting \(D_m>0\): \(n_{*m}\le n\) if and only if \(G_m\ge0\) if
  and only if \(x_m\le n\).
- Exact equality \(2^{K_m}=3^m\) cannot occur.

Thus on actual trajectories the candidate \(n_{*m}\le n\) is **not** a
new positivity constraint. In the only nontrivial regime it is exactly
"the orbit has already dropped to or below the start".

## The inequality \(n_*\le n\) is false

The finite-code pattern \(n_*\le R\) survived every prefix with
\(m\le8\) and \(k_i\le6\). On actual orbits it fails.

Smallest counterexample, **REFUTED EXACTLY**:

\[
n=165,\qquad m=17,\qquad
\mathbf{k}=(4,1,1,1,1,2,2,1,2,1,1,2,1,1,1,2,3),
\]

\[
K=27,\quad C=1106233681,\quad x=167,\quad
D=5077565,\quad G=-268435456,
\]

\[
n_*=\frac{1106233681}{5077565}>165=n=R(\mathbf{k}).
\]

The prefix is contracting (\(2^{27}>3^{17}\)) and still sits above the
start. Because \(165<2^{28}\), the cylinder minimum is \(n\) itself, so
this is also a counterexample to \(n_*\le R\) as a statement about
arbitrary finite codes. The earlier census missed it because it truncated
at \(m\le8\).

The first few further actual-orbit witnesses include \(171,231,257,259\).

A complete scan of odd \(n\le 10^6\) through at most \(250\) accelerated
steps found **148** contracting prefixes with \(x_m>n\). The smallest
start remains \(165\). A \(10^7\) scan was not recorded: the \(10^6\)
run already takes about two minutes in CPython, and the smallest witness
is independent of that bound.

## 2-adic compatibility

`required_start_residue` is the existing cylinder residue
\(R(\mathbf{k})\bmod 2^{K+1}\). Comparing it with \(n\bmod 2^{K+1}\)
reproduces nested-cylinder membership. Once the modulus exceeds \(n\),
the residue is uniquely \(n\) (**PROVED; LEAN VERIFIED** as unique
residue of a small start). This is the already-known stabilization /
zero-lift fact, not a new infinite-\(n\) constraint.

## Periodic codes

If a period-\(p\) word with data \((C,K)\) is realized by a positive odd
cycle point, then **PROVED; LEAN VERIFIED**

\[
n(2^K-3^p)=C.
\]

Expanding periods have \(C>0\) and \(2^K<3^p\), so the candidate is
negative and cannot be a positive cycle. The period \((2)\) yields the
candidate \(n=1\), which does realize \(T(1)=1\). The all-ones period is
expanding and has no positive candidate. Excluding a periodic code does
not prove Collatz.

## Stronger comparisons involving \(G\)

The only exact scaling that is always integral is

\[
\frac{G_m}{2^{K_m}}=n-x_m\in\mathbb Z.
\]

This restates the affine identity. Ratios \(G_m/3^m\), \(G_m/n\), and
\(G_m/C_m\) are the same data in other units. No independent lower bound
of the form \(G_m\ge c\cdot(\text{structural quantity})\) was derived
that is not already \(2^{K_m}(n-x_m)\).

## Non-contracting prefixes

An itinerary is called asymptotically non-contracting when
\(\liminf_m K_m/m\le\log_2 3\). Computations compare \(2^{K_m}\) and
\(3^m\) exactly, never a floating threshold.

Along expanding prefixes one has \(x_m>n\) automatically. That is
compatible with a fixed positive start: the orbit is allowed to rise.
The affine identities then say \(n_{*m}<0\) and \(A_m=B_m-n\) with
\(B_m=\lambda_m^{-1}x_m\). No contradiction with a single fixed \(n\)
was obtained.

Routes A–E of the milestone:

| Route | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| A. \(G_m\) must change sign | False as an obstruction: \(G\) starts at \(0\), goes negative on every \(k=1\) step, and is positive after descent. Sign changes are the stopping-time story. |
| B. \(n_{*m}\) exceeds \(n\) infinitely often | Equivalent to contracting prefixes with \(x_m>n\). This happens (witness \(165\)), but only finitely often on every checked trajectory that later falls below \(n\). Not an infinite obstruction. |
| C. \(A_m\) grows incompatibly | \(A_m\) is \(C_m/3^m\). No new constraint. |
| D. 2-adic precision | Reproduces the cylinder. |
| E. integrality plus positivity | The affine formula already encodes both. |

Finite experiments are **inconclusive** as a proof that
\(\liminf K_m/m\le\log_2 3\) is impossible. They are conclusive as a
refutation of \(n_*\le n\) / \(n_*\le R\).

## Special cases

- \(n=1\): period \((2)\), \(G=0\), \(n_*=1\).
- \(n=2\): even; accelerated \(T\) is undefined. No signed-\(T\) convention.
- \(n=3\): valuations \((1,4)\), first step expanding, then contracting with \(x=1<3\).
- \(n=7\): rises to \(11\) then falls; first contracting prefix already has \(x\le7\).
- \(n=27\): first contracting prefix at \(m=37\) with \(x=23<27\), so \(n_*\le n\) holds on this famous trajectory even though it fails for \(165\).
- All-ones period: expanding, no positive candidate.
- All-twos period: candidate \(1\).
- High-stopping examples \(41,703,6171\): first contracting prefixes occur after the orbit has dropped below the start, so they do not witness \(n_*>n\).

## Assessment

**Structural leverage, plus one concrete refutation, and no new
non-contracting obstruction.**

1. Exact new theorems: \(G=2^K(n-x)\); the \(G\) recurrence; \(A\) series
   equals \(C/3^m\); expanding \(\Rightarrow x>n\); periodic-code identity;
   unique residue of a start smaller than the modulus.
2. Lean-verified: those algebraic identities, \(2^K\ne3^m\) for \(m\ge1\),
   and the positivity-versus-gap distinction.
3. Recurrences for \(C\), \(A\), \(n_*\), and \(G\) are exact as above.
4. \(n_*\le n\) does **not** survive exhaustive search. Smallest
   counterexample: \(n=165\), \(m=17\). Among odd \(n\le 10^6\) there are
   \(148\) contracting prefixes with \(x>n\).
5. \(G\) has a useful exact sign-transparent recurrence, but it does not
   prove \(G\ge0\).
6. \(A_m\) provides no information beyond the exponent code.
7. No non-contracting fixed-\(n\) obstruction was found.
8. Rejected conjecture: \(n_*\le n\) (and, as a general finite-code
   statement, \(n_*\le R\)), witness \(165\).
9. Literature: dictionary with Kramer / exponent codes; the new angle is
   fixed-\(n\) affine-gap geometry, not a new 2-adic or 3-adic coordinate.
10. Blunt label: **structural leverage**. Clean identities, one exact
    refutation of a previously open census pattern, and no plausible
    asymptotic contradiction with \(\liminf K_m/m\le\log_2 3\).
