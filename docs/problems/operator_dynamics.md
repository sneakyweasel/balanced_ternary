## Problem

Whether the monoid generated by \(\{S,N,D,W\}\) has an exact composition
identity that is not a consequence of the identities already recorded
in [operator_algebra.md](../operator_algebra.md).

## Exact statement

Write \(S(n)=3n\), \(N(n)=-n\), \(D(n)=(n-a_0(n))/3\), and \(W\) for
canonical balanced-ternary reverse (OEIS A134028). Let \(K_3=W\circ W\).
The recorded exact identities include \(N\circ N=\mathrm{id}\),
\(D\circ S=\mathrm{id}\), \(N\) commutes with \(S,D,W,K_3\),
\(W\circ S=W\), \(W\circ W=K_3\), \(W^3=W\), \(K_3\) a projection, and
\(K_3\circ W=W\circ K_3=W\). \(S\circ D\) is not the identity.
\(D\) and \(W\) have unbounded defect.

Question: after reducing by those identities, does a new exact identity
remain among words of length at most 4 in the monoid generated by
\(\{S,N,D,W\}\), or is every such identity a consequence?

This is a question about maps \(\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}\). It is not
OpFrag (`discover_closed` is \(\{D,I_\pm,I_0,S,N\}\) only). It is not a
word-table Newman certificate: `WORD_SIMP` / `WORD_WN` / `WORD_WND`
already have unique syntactic normal forms and do not claim semantic
canonicity. The fragment is \(\{S,N,D,W\}\) only. No \(I_\pm\), \(M_2\),
\(H_2\), \(W_z\), \(W_t\), \(T\), Add, or Mul.

## Balanced-ternary formulation

Words are written in mathematical order: `W D N S` means
\(W\circ D\circ N\circ S\). Evaluation is `Composition.apply` on
\(\mathbb{Z}\). \(K_3\) appears only as the recorded rewrite of
\(W\circ W\), not as a generator.

## Why BT may be relevant

\(S\), \(D\), and \(W\) are the shift, LSD-drop, and reverse of the
canonical word. The question is whether those word operations impose
an extra integer identity beyond the recorded list.

## Candidate operations / invariants

- Recorded rewrite orientation on \(\{S,N,D,W,K_3\}\) — already

- Semantic equality of distinct normal forms on \(\mathbb{Z}\) —
  Phase-0 target.
- `WORD_WND` unique syntactic NF — **EXACT — HUMAN PROOF**, semantic

## Results

Phase-0 on words of length \(\le 4\) over \(\{S,N,D,W\}\):

- The recorded identities orient to a terminating list whose
  string-rewriting critical pairs join. Distinct normal forms are
  therefore not equational consequences of that list.
- 341 words, 77 normal forms. The rewrite is sound on the probe set.
- No two distinct normal forms agree as maps on the probe set
  (**COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED**). Every identity of this bounded class
  is a recorded consequence.
- Already-recorded non-identities stay distinct: \(W\circ W\ne\mathrm{id}\)
  at \(n=3\), \(W(3n)\ne 3W(n)\) at \(n=1\), \(S\circ D\ne\mathrm{id}\)
  at \(n=1\), \(D\circ W\ne W\circ D\) at \(n=10\). The production peak
  \(N\circ W\circ W\) joins to \(K_3\circ N\) once the recorded commute
  \(N\circ K_3\) is used (opt-in only; not installed in
  `WORD_REWRITE_RULES`).

Novelty: the recorded identities are already on the ledger. The bounded
negative scan is taxonomy, not a new theorem. No ledger row.
