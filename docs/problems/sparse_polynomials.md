[balanced_ternary_polynomials.md](../balanced_ternary_polynomials.md).

not a question. Extending the range is explicitly not a reason to
continue.

Best next question: is the factorization behaviour of \(P_n\) tied to
any balanced-ternary invariant of \(n\), rather than to its degree?
