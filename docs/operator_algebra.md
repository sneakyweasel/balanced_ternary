# Operator algebra

Composition is written in mathematical order: `W D N S` means
\(W\circ D\circ N\circ S\), i.e. apply \(S\), then \(N\), then \(D\), then
\(W\). Rewrite rules in `operator_algebra.py` are exact identities on the
intersection of domains. Identity/involution flags from the composition
census are only scans.

## Exact identities (PROVED)

1. \(N\circ N=\mathrm{id}\).
2. \(D\circ S=\mathrm{id}\). \(S\circ D(n)=n-a_0(n)\).
3. \(D\circ I_{\pm}=\mathrm{id}\).
4. \(H_2\circ M_2=\mathrm{id}\) on \(\mathbb{Z}\). \(M_2\circ H_2=\mathrm{id}\)
   on \(2\mathbb{Z}\).
5. \(H_3\circ S=\mathrm{id}\).
6. \(N\) commutes with \(S\), \(D\), \(M_2\), \(W\), \(W_z\), \(W_t\), \(K_3\).
7. \(S\) commutes with \(M_2\) and with \(W_z\). \(S\) does **not** commute
   with \(W\): \(W(S(n))=W(n)\) and \(S(W(n))=3W(n)\).
8. \(W\circ S=W\) and \(K_3\circ S=K_3\).
9. \(W\circ W=K_3\). Consequently \(W^3=W\), and \(K_3\) is a projection.
10. \(K_3\circ W=W\circ K_3=W\), because \(W(n)\) is 3-free for every \(n\).
11. \(W_z(3n)=3W_z(n)\), so \(W_z\) commutes with \(S\).

Lean verifies (1), (2) as \(D\circ S=\mathrm{id}\) on words, (6) for \(S\) and
\(D\), (8) as `W_after_S`, and the witness \(W(3)=1\).

## REFUTED

- \(W\circ W=\mathrm{id}\) on \(\mathbb{Z}\). Witness \(n=3\).
- \(W(3n)=3W(n)\). Same witness.
- \(S\circ D=\mathrm{id}\) on \(\mathbb{Z}\). Fails precisely when \(a_0\neq 0\).

\(D\circ W\) drops the MSD of \(n\) (the LSD of \(W(n)\)). \(W\circ D\) drops
the LSD of \(n\) and then reverses. They agree iff the canonical length is
at most 1.

## Finite-order elements

In the monoid generated by these maps (partial maps on \(\mathbb{Z}\)):

- involutions: \(N\), \(W_z\), \(W_t\)
- projection: \(K_3\)
- regular of index 2: \(W\), because \(W^3=W\) but \(W^2\neq W\)
- \(S\) and \(M_2\) have infinite order
- \(D\) is nilpotent on each integer (reaches 0 in finitely many steps) but
  not nilpotent as an operator on \(\mathbb{Z}\)

No unexpected torsion was found among length-3 words of
\(\{S,N,D,W,W_z,W_t,M_2,H_2\}\) beyond consequences of the rewrite list.
That census is **VERIFIED COMPUTATIONALLY** on a bounded sample, not a
classification of the free product.

## Dynamics (not Collatz)

- \(D\): unique fixed point \(0\). Every orbit reaches 0. Not a chaotic
  system; it is reading the word.
- \(W\): fixed points are the balanced-ternary palindromes (A134027).
  On \(3\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}\) the orbit is \(n\mapsto W(n)\mapsto K_3(n)\)
  and then a 2-cycle or a palindromic fixed point of \(W\).
- \(W_t\), \(W_z\): period 1 or 2 everywhere.
- \(N\circ W\): same orbits as \(W\) up to global sign.

Nothing here produces an independent dynamical obstruction to Collatz, and
none was sought.
