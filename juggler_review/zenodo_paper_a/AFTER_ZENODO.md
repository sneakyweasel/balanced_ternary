# After the DOI exists

You do not need arXiv to have a citable Paper A. The DOI is enough
for Paper B’s reference [22] and for any later journal.

## Journals (read this before emailing)

**Integers** (`integersjournal@gmail.com`) is the right *subject*
for a short cycle census, and it charges nothing. It is the wrong
*policy* for this manuscript as written. Their rule is: they will
not consider an article that uses AI to produce mathematics, code,
bibliographic information, or other content — only basic
grammar/spelling tools. The note already says large language models
were used extensively while drafting, for Lean statements, and for
literature records. You cannot honestly tick their AI box without
rewriting the paper so that statement is false. Do not send a
cover letter that pretends otherwise.

**Journal of Integer Sequences** wants LaTeX, not PDF, and wants
the subject tied to OEIS (A094683, A007320, A094716 are already
cited). Their rule is narrower: do not use an LLM to write the
English words; locating references is allowed if you checked each
one; suggested proofs must be credited in the text. The existing
acknowledgment is a start. They may still reject on “the words
must be your own.” If you try JIS, keep the acknowledgment, send
the `.md`/`.tex` source, and use the subject line exactly

    Submission to the Journal of Integer Sequences

Do not send Paper B.

**Larger journals** (*Acta Arithmetica*, *J. Number Theory*) will
treat this as a short note at best. Wait for the DOI and for a
reader. None of them replaces Zenodo this week.

## arXiv later

Once someone in `math.NT` has the DOI and is willing, send them
the endorsement link from a draft submission. Until then, leave
arXiv alone. Do not open a second arXiv account.
