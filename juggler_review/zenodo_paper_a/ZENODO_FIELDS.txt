DESCRIPTION (paste as HTML)

<p>The Juggler map is the nonlinear integer map J(n) = floor(sqrt(n)) if n is even, and J(n) = floor(n^{3/2}) if n is odd. It is conjectured that every positive integer eventually reaches 1.</p>
<p>A realized parity word obeys a power envelope, so a cycle word is formally expanding. Floor remainders must finance the surplus 3^o &minus; 2^L. At a cycle minimum this yields the financing inequality n log n &middot; (3^o &minus; 2^L) &le; L &middot; 3^o. This inequality is the main theorem; every numerical period bound is an instantiation of it, or of its walk-charge refinement, at a certified descent floor. Combined with the independently verified descent that every start through 10^6 reaches 1, any nontrivial cycle has period at least 25781. A walk-charge envelope &mdash; transport of the floor losses to a reduced base, identification of the extremal exponent walk as a rotation word, and a Denjoy&ndash;Koksma bound over certified Ostrowski blocks, census-free on the window [50508, 301994) &mdash; extends the exclusion at a certified laboratory floor N0 = 26254995 to period at least 176251. The main numerical result evaluates the same certified kill criterion at a second certified floor, N0 = 162849448, on the surviving lengths, including those beyond the census-free window: any nontrivial Juggler cycle has period at least 478245. A separate inverse-cell analysis shows that every nontrivial cycle has at least four even letters and hence period at least eleven, with no descent floor. The core lemmas are mechanized in Lean 4; the descent floors and the per-length kill tables are independently certified computations, and the paper does not claim to be formally verified as a whole.</p>
