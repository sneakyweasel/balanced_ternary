DESCRIPTION (paste as HTML)

<p>The Juggler map is the nonlinear integer map J(n) = floor(sqrt(n)) if n is even, and J(n) = floor(n^{3/2}) if n is odd. It is conjectured that every positive integer eventually reaches 1. This note does not prove that conjecture.</p>
<p>A realized parity word w obeys the power envelope (J^{|w|}(n))^{2^{|w|}} &le; n^{3^{#O(w)}}, so a cycle word is formally expanding. Inverse cells and two next-square thresholds give an elementary census: there is no nontrivial cycle of length at most seven. Leftover even-count families then assemble: no cycle word has fewer than four even letters, so a nontrivial cycle has period at least eleven.</p>
<p>The same envelope, with floor remainders kept, is an exact global defect. Around a cycle the remainders must finance the formal surplus 3^o &minus; 2^L. The resulting inequality at a cycle minimum, together with a verified descent floor through 10^6, excludes every period at most 1053, and every period at most 10^5 outside an explicit set of 397 near-convergent lengths of ln 2 / ln 3. The first record survivor is length 1054. No density result is stated or used.</p>
