DESCRIPTION (paste as HTML)

<p>The Juggler map is the nonlinear integer map J(n) = floor(sqrt(n)) if n is even, and J(n) = floor(n^{3/2}) if n is odd. It is conjectured that every positive integer eventually reaches 1. This note does not prove that conjecture, and it does not exclude every cycle.</p>
<p>A realized parity word obeys a power envelope, so a cycle word is formally expanding. Floor remainders must finance the surplus 3^o &minus; 2^L. At a cycle minimum this yields n log n &middot; (3^o &minus; 2^L) &le; L &middot; 3^o. Combined with the published verification that every start through 10^6 reaches 1, and with the parity-aware floor-error budget, there is no nontrivial cycle of length at most 25780. Through length 10^5, only an explicit set of 141 lengths remains admissible to the bound. A separate finite-word analysis shows that every nontrivial cycle has at least four even letters and hence period at least eleven.</p>
<p>2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 11B83, 37P99, 11Y55.</p>
