# Juggler position-dependent cycle finance

Odd-run height refinement of joint-minima m-finance.
Not a new paper. Not a halt theorem. No new Lean.

Regenerate with `python -m research.juggler_sequence.cycle_position_finance`.

`l84_floors.json` is the leftover-84 kill-floor comparison (global vs
joint-minima vs height law, constants 1 and 6/5) plus orbit-cost
counts for residual-floor campaigns. The 4756 global-finance campaign
is PARK.

`leftover_at_floors.json` is the Phase-0 leftover census at residual
floors 1981 and 4756. Height at 1981 renames the leftover to 168.
Regenerate with `python -m research.juggler_sequence.cycle_l84_residual_floor`.
